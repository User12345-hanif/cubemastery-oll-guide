2-Look OLL: The 10 Algorithms to Orient the Last Layer

OLL, Orientation of the Last Layer, is the CFOP step that makes the whole top face one colour. You do not need all 57 OLL cases to start. 2-Look OLL does it in two looks, with just 10 algorithms: first orient the edges to make the yellow cross, then orient the corners to finish the yellow face.

Step 1: Orient the edges

  • Line: F (R U R' U') F'
  • L-Shape: f (R U R' U') f'
  • Dot: F (R U R' U') F' then f (R U R' U') f'

After this step, the yellow cross is formed. If it is already formed, skip to Step 2.

Step 2: Orient the corners

  • Sune: R U R' U R U2 R'
  • Anti-Sune: (R' U' R) U' (R' U2 R)
  • Pi: f (R U R' U') f' F (R U R' U') F'
  • Headlights: R2' D R' U2 R D' R' U2 R'
  • Chameleon: r U R' U' r' F R F'
  • Bowtie: F' r U R' U' r' F R
  • H: F (R U R' U') (R U R' U') (R U R' U') F'

Learn these in short, regular sessions, and practise recognition first: look at the yellow pattern on top, match it, hold the cube as the guide shows, then execute the algorithm once. Mastering these 10 gives you a clear foundation before expanding toward the full 57-case set over time.
